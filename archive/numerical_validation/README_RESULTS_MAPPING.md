## Script Descriptions

- **experimental_strategy_validation.py**: Implements and tests classical strategies S1–S4 and quantum MBQC for Boolean functions (AND, OR, XOR, etc.), measuring success probabilities and comparing to theoretical bounds.
- **experimental_strategy_validation_v2.py**: Improved version with stricter physical constraints and corrected quantum MBQC modeling.
- **experimental_validation_physics.py**: Physics-constrained version, ensuring only physically realizable classical strategies are tested (no input-dependent cheating).
- **gap_filling_validations.py**: Implements missing validations: contextual fraction, gflow, treewidth, and feed-forward function checks.
- **comprehensive_math_validation.py**: Validates all mathematical claims (commutators, dimensions, stabilizer tableau, etc.).
- **validated_quantum_classical_comparison.py**: Directly compares quantum and classical (Mueller/tensor) results for gates, circuits, and contextuality.
- **physical_mbqc_simulation.py**: Simulates quantum MBQC and classical optics (Jones/Mueller calculus) for physical system comparison.
- **extended_qubit_validation.py**: Runs extended validation for larger system sizes.
- **classical_error_correction_scalability.py**: Analyzes error correction scalability for classical emulation.
- **enhanced_quantum_classical_benchmark.py**: Runs enhanced quantum/classical benchmarks.
- **rigorous_mathematical_validation.py**: Provides rigorous mathematical validation of key theorems.

## Embedding in the Presentation

- The results from `experimental_validation_results.json` (and its v2/v3/physics versions) are embedded in the presentation slides on classical emulation strategies, success bounds, and quantum advantage.
- The presentation transparently notes that some classical strategies exceed their theoretical bounds in experiment, as shown in these files.
- All mathematical and structural claims are validated by the results in `comprehensive_validation_results.json` and `complete_validation_results.json`.
- Direct quantum/classical comparisons and contextuality tests are validated by `validated_comparison_results.json`.
